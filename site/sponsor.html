Sponsor Winnow

One sponsored message, shown inside Winnow's own toolbar popup and settings page, to people who installed a tool for checking whether they are being lied to. No tracking, no targeting, and no data about who saw it — including for you.

What you get

  • A headline, a line of body copy and your name, rendered as text with your link. Up to 80, 140 and 40 characters. Optionally a small square image.
  • The slot to yourself for the period, or a share of it if more than one sponsor is running.
  • A link you control. The destination is a redirect on this domain, so you can change where it points, and count clicks, without waiting on an extension update.

What you do not get, and cannot buy

Winnow's product is telling people when a rating has been bought. It cannot sell the same thing quietly at the back. These are not negotiable at any price.

  • No targeting. The request that fetches your message contains three values: a schema version, which of the two windows is asking, and the formats it can draw. There is no field for a product, a page, a search term or a user. You cannot be shown against a particular listing, because nothing knows what listing is open.
  • No audience data. No demographics, no interests, no click-level reporting tied to a person, because none of it is collected to report.
  • No placement on Amazon. The message never renders on a product page and never renders next to a grade.
  • No influence on any grade, ever. If you sell on Amazon, sponsoring Winnow will not change what Winnow says about your listings, and we will tell you so in writing before taking your money.
  • No pixels, scripts or iframes. The extension renders text and a validated image URL. Anything resembling executable content is rejected outright by the validator, not sanitised.

What the reader sees

A labelled block: the word Sponsored, your name beside it, your headline and body, and a line underneath reading "Chosen without knowing which product you are viewing." Readers can switch sponsorship off entirely in Settings, and some will. That is part of the deal.

What it costs

Ask. Winnow's install base is small and public — you can read it on the Chrome Web Store listing before you talk to us. Quoting a rate card against an audience you can go and count yourself would be theatre.

Flat monthly, paid in advance, invoiced. We are not going to sell you impressions we cannot verify to you, and we do not run an auction.

Who this suits

People who install a review-integrity tool skew toward the careful end of the market: they read permissions, they check before buying, and they are hostile to being tracked. If your product survives that kind of attention, this is a good room. If it depends on not being looked at closely, it is the wrong one.

Get in touch

Open an issue at github.com/Cristians68/Winnow/issues titled "Sponsorship" — or email the address on the Chrome Web Store listing. Tell us what you would want the message to say and roughly how long you would want it running.
